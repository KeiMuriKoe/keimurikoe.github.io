Privacy Policy

Block Artist: Color Puzzle (the “Game”, package com.playcus.BlockArtist)

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play the Game, why it is collected, who it is shared with and what choices you have. The Game is developed by RevoStudio (“we”, “us”).

1. Who is responsible

RevoStudio is responsible for the processing of personal data described in this policy. Contact: revostudio.gamedev@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect and why

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The Game uses the following data:

  • Game progress and settings. Your progress, coins, purchased items and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is saved to your Google account (Google Play Games “Saved Games”) so you can restore it on another device.
  • Game identifiers. The Game creates a random identifier for your installation. It is not linked to your real identity and is used to connect analytics and crash reports from the same installation.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation (product, order identifier and purchase token) to deliver the item. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Usage analytics. With Google Analytics for Firebase we collect how the Game is used, for example levels started and completed, tutorial steps, settings changes and purchase attempts, together with device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), an app instance identifier and approximate location (country or region derived from your IP address). We use this to understand and improve the Game.
  • Crash and error reports. With Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase we collect crash reports, error messages from the Game, the state of the device at the time of the error (for example free memory and OS version) and an installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix problems.
  • Advertising. The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects the advertising identifier of your device, your IP address, device information and your interactions with ads. Personalized ads are shown only if you agree to them where consent is required (see section 4).

3. Service providers

The services above are provided by Google LLC and its affiliates, which process the data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own terms:

  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob and the advertising partners selected by Google

More information: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Privacy and Security in Firebase.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized advertising and the related data processing. If you decline, ads are not personalized. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings in the Game settings.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can delete your game progress with Delete progress in the Game settings. Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your device. Data saved in Google Play Games can be deleted in the Google Play Games settings.
  • To ask us to delete analytics or crash data related to your installation, write to us at the address above.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK)

  • Providing the Game, saving progress and delivering purchases: performance of our agreement with you.
  • Analytics, crash and error reports, non-personalized ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in running, improving and funding the Game.
  • Personalized advertising: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

6. Data retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. Game progress is kept until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own policies.

7. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google uses appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California may have similar rights under the CCPA/CPRA. To use these rights, contact us at revostudio.gamedev@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Data is transmitted to Google services over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use the security measures provided by these services.

11. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the Game or on its Google Play page.
